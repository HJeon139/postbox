# Library: how a book reaches a reader

- **Idea:** Two separate paths, each with its own check, meet at the branch shelf, where the reader’s path begins.
- **For:** The library network’s staff and readers.
- **Question:** Where do new books come from, how are they checked, and how do readers get them?
- **Facts** (synthetic, from the [#206 field notes, Nov 4](https://github.com/HJeon139/postbox/issues/206#issuecomment-6644256875)):
  - New books arrive two ways: publisher orders and donations.
  - Orders are cataloged at the central branch; donations get a condition check at the bindery. Different teams run each check.
  - Both paths end on a branch shelf.
  - A reader reserves a book online and picks it up at a branch.
- **Shape:** Flow: two sources merge at one place, and a separate path leads out.
- **Must show:** the two checks as separate steps on separate paths; both paths reaching the shelf; the reader’s path starting at the shelf.
- **Traps:** one “checks” step for both paths; sentences in the boxes; animation of books moving, since nothing in the question is about time.
