# The water cycle

- **Idea:** The same water keeps moving in a loop: the Sun lifts it as vapor, it falls as rain and snow, and gravity carries it back to the sea, over land or underground.
- **For:** A middle-school student.
- **Question:** Where does rain come from, and where does it go?
- **Facts** ([USGS Water Science School, “The water cycle”](https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/water-science-school/science/water-cycle)):
  - Water moves between the atmosphere and the surface through evaporation, evapotranspiration and precipitation.
  - Condensation forms clouds; precipitation falls as rain and snow; snowmelt, surface runoff, streamflow, infiltration and groundwater flow carry it back.
  - Oceans store 96% of all water on Earth.
- **Shape:** Cycle.
- **Must show:** the loop closing back to the ocean; what drives the up leg (the Sun) and the down legs (gravity); that most of the water sits in the ocean.
- **Traps:** a landscape full of decoration where arrows point everywhere and the loop doesn’t read; a list of process names around a picture.
