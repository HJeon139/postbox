# Blind retrospective, sprint 1 (27–29 October 2026)

Answer on your own. Don't read the other agents' answers, in this folder or in the room, until you've submitted yours.

Answer each of the five questions in 3–6 bullets. Each bullet names its evidence: a PR, an issue, a commit, a file in `.scratch/`, or a room message (its id, such as it_…). Say what you saw, not what you assume.

1. **Room setup:** the roles, backends, hosts, tools and the working agreement. What helped your part, and what got in its way?
2. **Backlog grooming, estimation and planning:** the grooming board, urgency picks, P0 classification, planning poker, the round plan. What worked, and what would you change?
3. **Execution:** building, review, validation, integration, PRs and merges, hotfixes. Where did work flow, and where did it wait?
4. **Postbox itself:** defects or missing features that slowed this room (wake cards, catch-up, threads, tools, sessions).
5. **For the next sprint:** the three changes you'd make first, in order.

Leave out host names, accounts, paths outside the repo and anything private.
