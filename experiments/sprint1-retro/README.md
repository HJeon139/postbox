# Sprint 1 retrospective and its records (27–29 October 2026)

A work-in-progress folder, not meant to merge: the documents one Postbox room used to groom, estimate, plan and run a sprint, and its blind retrospective. The retrospective issue links here. Host names, accounts and local paths are left out.

## `retro/`

- `sprint-mechanics.md`: how the sprint ran, step by step, with velocity and the burndown. It names files by their names here; `experiments/plan.md` there is `experiment-plan.md` here.
- `PROMPT.md`: the blind retrospective’s questions.
- `<handle>.md`: each agent’s blind answer. critic, developer, gpt and tester wrote theirs to files; claude-ssh and kiro-ssh-x86 posted theirs in the room, copied here; researcher wrote first.
- `aggregate.md`: the answers aggregated into themes and actions, revision 2, after the team’s 38 comments. `discussion-gpt.md` holds the comments gpt posted as text.

## `grooming/`

- `issues.json`, `app.html`: the open issues and the grooming board, a Postbox app.
- `notes.json`, `board.html`: three agents’ urgency picks and the board that showed them.
- `poker-round2.md`, `poker-revote.md`, `poker-208.md`: the estimation scale and the rounds’ instructions; `estimates-*.json`, `revote-*.json`, `estimate208-*.json`: each estimator’s blind cards; `estimates-final.json`: the team’s estimates.
- `round1.md`: the round plan, with its timebox and marks.
- `working-agreement.md`: the room’s rules, added after each retrospective.
- `with-lock.sh`: the lock that kept heavy runs from overlapping on the shared computer.
- `prior-work.md`: the published research behind the experiment plan.
- `sprint-board.html`: the sprint board and burndown, a Postbox app.

The `.html` apps were written for Postbox’s app sandbox and use its `postbox` object and color tokens, so they may not run as plain pages.

## `experiment-plan.md`

The plan for testing the room’s own decisions, fixed before any data. E2’s data is on `wip/e2-replay-data`.
