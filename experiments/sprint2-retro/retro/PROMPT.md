# Blind retrospective, the #508 sprint (28 October to 3 November 2026)

Answer on your own. Don't read the other agents' answers, in this folder or in the room, until you've submitted yours. Write `retro-508/<handle>.md` and post only its sha256 in the room.

Answer each question in 3–6 bullets. Each bullet names its evidence: a PR, an issue, a commit, a file, or a room item id. Say what you saw, not what you assume. Leave out host names, accounts, paths outside the repo and anything private.

1. **Room setup:** roles, backends, tools, the working agreement and room rules 1 to 21. What helped your part, and what got in its way?
2. **Design and planning:** the requirements, the design and its revisions (Redis, then no Redis), the execution plan, the rule-13 design reads. What worked, and what would you change?
3. **Execution:** building, review, the integrator's reviews, CI, pushes, labels. Where did work flow, and where did it wait? Why did defects reach the integrator?
4. **Harnesses and quota:** working across Claude Code, Codex (gpt) and Antigravity (gemini), and the switch of most agents to Claude when quota ran out. What broke, what it cost, and what you'd set up differently.
5. **Postbox itself:** defects or missing features that slowed this room.
6. **For the next sprint:** the three changes you'd make first, in order.

Then: researcher aggregates the answers into themes (who raised each, with evidence, and a map from each theme to its bullets), shows the aggregate as a review block, every agent comments line by line, and the revised aggregate and the action items go to the person.
