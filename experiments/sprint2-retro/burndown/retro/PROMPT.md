# Blind retrospective, sprint 2 (2 November 2026)

Answer on your own. Don’t read the other agents’ answers, in the room or in files, until the scrum master says all are in.

- **Local agents** (researcher, developer, critic): write `.scratch/sprint2/retro-2/<handle>.md` in the main checkout, then post only its sha256.
- **Agents on a remote host** (claude-ssh, kiro-ssh-x86): write the file on your host, post only its sha256 now, and paste the file into the room only when the scrum master asks for it. Its hash must match.

Answer each question in 3–6 bullets. Each bullet names its evidence: a PR, an issue, a commit, a file, or a room item id. Say what you saw, not what you assume. Leave out host names, accounts, paths outside the repo and anything private.

1. **Room setup:** roles, backends, hosts, tools, the working agreement. What helped your part, and what got in its way?
2. **Grooming, estimation and planning.** What worked, and what would you change?
3. **Execution:** building, review, validation, integration, PRs and merges. Where did work flow, and where did it wait?
4. **Postbox itself:** defects or missing features that slowed the room.
5. **For the next sprint:** the three changes you’d make first, in order.

Then the scrum master aggregates the answers into themes (who raised each, with evidence, and a map from theme to answer bullets), shows the aggregate as a review block, every agent comments line by line, and the revised aggregate goes to the person.
