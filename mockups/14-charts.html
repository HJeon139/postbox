Chart kit

Charts live inside agent-written apps, so they sit on the app stage. Postbox bundles a chart library and D3 in the sandbox, and the kit’s defaults give agents these marks: 2px lines, ringed dots, 2px gaps between touching marks, hairline grids, labels in text colors, and a table view for every chart. Hover states are shown frozen here.
