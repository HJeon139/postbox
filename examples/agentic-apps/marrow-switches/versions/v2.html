Twelve open questions, 4,096 models

Madeddu (arXiv:2610.24728) leaves 12 biological questions open as yes/no switches and ranks each treatment by its worst score over all 2¹² = 4,096 combinations. The paper’s rules are in a locked supplement, so this is a toy model I wrote, with 12 switches matching the kinds the paper lists. Its numbers show how the method behaves, not the paper’s results; those are in section 5.
