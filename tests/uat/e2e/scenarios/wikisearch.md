# Wiki search: picking an approach

- persona: A backend engineer adding search to an internal wiki of about 20,000 pages that change daily. Knows databases well and information retrieval barely. Practical, writes short messages, and wants a recommendation but likes to make the call.
- agents: researcher
- models: persona
- project: Wiki search
- conversation: Picking a search approach

Ask @researcher a question with several reasonable answers: how to add search to the wiki. Write as you would to a colleague, and don’t ask for any particular format. Answer whatever it asks the way it lets you, and notice whether you could pick an answer with a click or had to type one out.

## Phase 1: Several ways to do it

Ask which approach to use for the wiki’s search: keyword search, embeddings, or a mix, for 20,000 pages that change daily and a team of three. Let it lay out the options, then pick one the way it offers.

## Phase 2: What to settle first

Ask what you need to decide before building the approach you picked: how often to reindex, how to rank, and how you’ll know it works. If it has questions for you, answer them where it asks. End when you know what to build first.
