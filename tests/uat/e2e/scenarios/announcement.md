# Launch announcement: a draft to approve

- persona: A product manager announcing the new wiki search to the whole company. Busy, careful about tone and claims, writes short messages, and signs off on anything before it goes out.
- agents: researcher
- models: persona
- project: Launch notes
- conversation: Wiki search announcement

Ask @researcher for a draft you’ll approve before it’s sent. Write as you would to a colleague, and don’t ask for any particular format. Give your feedback the way it lets you, and notice whether you could comment on the draft where it is or had to quote it back in a message.

## Phase 1: The first draft

Ask for a short announcement of the new wiki search for the company’s weekly email, about 200 words: what it does, how to try it, and who to contact. Say you’ll approve it before it goes out. If it has questions for you first, answer them where it asks. Then give feedback on the draft: the opening is too slow, and one sentence overstates what the search can do.

## Phase 2: Revised and approved

Ask for a revision with your feedback, and check you can tell what changed. Approve it when it reads right, or ask for one more change first.
