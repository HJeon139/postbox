# Held-out experiment, an idea, baseline

- persona: A curious commuter who has sat in highway jams that cleared with nothing to show for them. Writes short messages, and can’t say what a good picture looks like until they see one.
- agents: researcher
- models: persona
- judge: visual
- steps: 6
- project: Questions
- conversation: Jams out of nowhere

This measures how an agent explains with a picture on one of the held-out briefs (designs/skills/visual-explainer/briefs/heldout/), written apart from the skill’s tuning for the experiment on the visual mechanisms. **Baseline:** no skill, the same words, to compare with the run that has it. The runs are scored afterward, with the takeaway test on the first visual’s screenshot; your job is to ask as this person would. Don’t name a chart type, a library, Postbox’s tools, the word “app” or the skill.

## Phase 1: Ask

Send, word for word: “Why do traffic jams show up on the highway when there’s no accident, no roadwork, nothing? Can you show me how that happens?” Then wait until the agent has finished and its visual is on screen.

Pass if a visual is on screen. Note, without failing the phase, whether it’s an app, a Markdown diagram or a table, and what it shows first.

## Phase 2: One follow-up

Ask, word for word: “So when I’m stuck in one, is the jam moving along with me?” Then wait for the reply.

Pass if the agent answers by pointing at the visual or changing it. Note whether it answered from the picture or only in prose.
